## Interests in deceased estates

Interests in deceased estates
Where the bankrupt has an interest in a deceased estate that arises before or during bankruptcy, this interest vests in the trustee.
Where the deceased died before the bankrupt was discharged from bankruptcy, the interest vests – this is the case even if a distribution from the deceased estate doesn’t occur until after discharge.
Deceased estate interests involving life tenancies
If the bankrupt has an interest in a deceased estate that includes property that is subject to a life tenancy, the trustee is bound by that.  The executors are asked to advise the trustee when the terms of the will have been complied with and the interest is able to be dealt with.
